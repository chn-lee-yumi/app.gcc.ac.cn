Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

AI Image Editor (“we”, “us” or “our”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the rights you have in relation to that information.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the AI Image Editor mobile application and related services. By using AI Image Editor you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. You can browse the app without signing in. Signing in with Apple is required only to generate images with points and to buy points.

2. Information we collect

  • Photos and prompts you submit: The photos, text prompts, and model settings you submit to generate an edited image. These are sent only when you tap Generate, after you have given the consent described in section 5.
  • Account identifier: If you sign in with Apple, we receive the identifier that Apple provides for your Apple account. We use it to link your points, purchases, and generation history to your account. We do not receive or store your Apple password.
  • Purchase information: Purchase and subscription records, including the App Store transaction identifiers needed to deliver purchased points. Payments are processed by Apple, and we do not receive your payment card details.
  • Usage and diagnostic data: Product interaction events (for example, which features you use and where errors occur), app version, and device information, collected by Google Firebase Analytics.
  • Advertising data: If you watch rewarded ads to earn points, Google AdMob receives ad request and impression data, and your account identifier so that we can verify the reward. AI Image Editor does not request App Tracking Transparency permission and does not access the advertising identifier (IDFA). We do not use this data to track you across other apps and websites. AdMob may collect other device identifiers as described in Google’s policies.

3. Face data

AI Image Editor does not detect, recognize, identify, or analyze faces. We do not create facial templates, face maps, or other biometric identifiers, and we do not use face data to identify or track anyone.

Photos you submit may show people, including their faces, because they are part of the image you want to edit. These photos are processed only as image content to generate the result you request, in the same way as any other photo. They are not used for face recognition, profiling, or advertising. Photos are handled as described in sections 5 and 6, including the sharing with the AI service providers listed in section 5.

4. How we use your information

We use the collected information only to provide and improve AI Image Editor:

  • Generate and deliver edited images;
  • Manage accounts, points, and purchases;
  • Record your consent choices and honor withdrawal;
  • Show rewarded advertising and measure its performance;
  • Detect and prevent misuse of our services;
  • Respond to support requests;
  • Analyze product usage trends and improve app quality.

5. Sharing with third-party AI services and your consent

Before your first generation, the app asks for your permission to send your photos and prompts to third-party AI services. When you give permission, the app sends only the photo(s) you select, your text prompt, and the model settings for your request. It does not send your Apple account identifier, name, or email to these services.

  • Cloudflare, Inc. (Workers AI): runs the AI models that generate your edited image.
  • Black Forest Labs (FLUX models): generates images for some edit requests, and may receive the request through Cloudflare.

Each provider processes the data under its own terms and privacy policy. We do not sell your personal data. Google Firebase Analytics and Google AdMob (section 2) are provided by Google. Purchases are processed by Apple under Apple’s policies.

You can withdraw this consent at any time on the Points screen by choosing “Withdraw AI photo processing consent”. After you withdraw, your photos are not sent to these services until you give consent again.

6. Data storage, retention, and security

Photos you submit and the generated results are stored on our servers located in the United States. We delete them from our servers within 24 hours. Generated results you keep are also saved on your device, and you can delete them in the app at any time.

Account and purchase records are kept while your account is active. When you delete your account in the app, we delete your account data and points. Purchase records that Apple keeps are governed by Apple.

We use industry-standard technical measures, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), to protect data in transit and on our servers.

7. Children

AI Image Editor is not intended for children under 15. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we discover such data, it will be promptly deleted.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights such as:

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal data;
  • Delete your account in the app (Points screen, “Delete Account”);
  • Restrict or object to certain processing;
  • Request a copy of your personal data;
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, including AI photo processing (Points screen).

To exercise other rights, contact us at the information below. We will respond in accordance with applicable law.

9. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Significant changes will be notified through the app or other appropriate means. Continued use of AI Image Editor after updates implies acceptance of the revised policy.
